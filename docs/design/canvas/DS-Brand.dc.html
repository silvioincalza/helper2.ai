Writing

  • Talk like a knowledgeable colleague: “Typing searches every source.”
  • Buttons say what happens: Search, Ask the guide, Save, Copy prompt, Try again.
  • Errors say what went wrong and what to do next, without apology.
  • Never invent popularity, dates or facts. Mock content uses brackets: [stated date].
  • Metadata reads source first, then date: “GitHub · Oct 21, 2026”.
  • A fact no source gave reads Unknown.

Icons

Small 14 px stroke glyphs beside words, never alone: check, cross, clock, pause, warn, info, bookmark, external link, refresh. “→” for go further, “↗” for an external source. No emoji. An icon-only button, such as close ×, has an accessible label.
