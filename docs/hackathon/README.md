on Devpost, track **Best Apps and Agents**.

## Rules this project must meet

From the [Official Rules](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules):

- The app makes a **runtime call to the Nebius Token Factory inference API**: every guide question does (plan, choose,
  reason check), and so does the Scout's enrichment. See [How Nemotron and Token Factory are used](../../README.md#how-nemotron-and-token-factory-are-used).
- It uses at least one **NVIDIA open model**: Nemotron 3 Nano and Nemotron 3 Super, plus `nemotron-3.5-embed-1b` for
  vector search.
- It is a genuine attempt at the **Best Apps and Agents** track: a multi-step workflow (plan, search, choose, validate).
- The demo stays free and unrestricted until the end of judging.
- The video is public, under 3 minutes, shows the app working, and names Nebius Token Factory and Nemotron.
- The repository is public, under an open-source license (Apache-2.0), with setup steps in the README.
- All materials are in English.
