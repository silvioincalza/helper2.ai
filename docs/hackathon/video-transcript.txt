Hi! I'm Silvio Incalza, a software engineer from Zurich, and I'm really excited to show you Helper2.ai: a metasearch engine for the whole AI ecosystem, that only tells you what it can prove!

Every single day there are new AI tools, models, MCP servers and papers, scattered across dozens of sites. Ask a chatbot and you get a fast answer, but it might name a tool that doesn't even exist! So I asked myself: what if an AI search engine could only tell you what it can prove? Let me show you.

One box. One search across the whole AI ecosystem: GitHub, Hugging Face, npm, arXiv, the Official MCP Registry and live AI news, all at once! Every result tells you where it came from and when. A source fails? A date is missing? The page tells you. It never, ever guesses.

Now the fun part: I just describe what I need, in my own words, and ask the guide. This is where NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory shines! Nemotron Nano turns my words, in any language, into a search plan. We find real candidates. Then Nemotron 3 Super picks the best ones, and for each one it must quote the project's own text. And here's the magic: code checks every quote, word for word. Look, one suggestion was removed, because the evidence didn't back it up! A second Super call even checks that every reason stays within its quote.

But finding a tool is only half the job! Hit Guide me, and you get a ready-to-paste setup for nineteen AI clients, straight from the MCP Registry or the project's README. No secrets filled in, and every command checked against the README. Copy, paste, done!

It speaks any language, and it shows upcoming AI events, only when a source actually states the dates.

And it's all open source, Apache-2.0, live right now at helper2.ai, no account needed! Spring Boot, Spring AI, React, more than five hundred backend tests, running on Kubernetes with full monitoring. Funded by donations, partnerships and crowdfunding, never by paid rankings.

Helper2.ai: the AI ecosystem metasearch engine that only tells you what it can prove. Try it today, and thanks for watching!
